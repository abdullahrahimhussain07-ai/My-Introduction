## About Me

I'm a university student who likes taking projects from a rough idea all the way to something people can actually use — logic, structure, and the details that tie it together. Most of my current work centers on data-heavy systems and applied engineering.

I work comfortably in both **English** and **Urdu**, and I'm especially interested in building tools that solve real problems.

- 🎓 Currently a university student, studying Robotics at PAF-IAST
- 🛠️ Focused on applied engineering, data-driven tools, and problem-solving
- 🌍 Based in Saudi Arabia and Pakistan
- 📈 Always tinkering with something outside of class

## 🚀 Projects

### Predictive Maintenance: Industrial ML for Fault Detection
Applied machine learning to industrial sensor data to predict equipment faults before they happen, cutting down on unplanned downtime.

### Machine Learning for Automotive Safety
